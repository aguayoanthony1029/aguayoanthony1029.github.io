Data & backup

Where are my bets stored?

Your tracker data is stored on your device. When private iCloud synchronization is available and enabled, it may also sync through your personal iCloud account. Nuke support cannot browse your private tracker records.

Getting started

Do I need an account?

No Nuke or Discord login is required to use the unit-tracking tools. Enter your unit value and begin logging bets. Community membership links are optional and open outside the app.

Calculations

Why did my units change?

Wins, losses, pushes, and voids affect net units differently. Confirm the odds, risk units, and final result on the bet before saving. Pending bets are not counted as settled profit or loss.

Troubleshooting

Something does not look right

  • Confirm you are running the latest App Store version.
  • Close and reopen the app.
  • Check that the correct date range is selected.
  • Verify the bet's odds, stake, and result.
  • Email support before deleting the app if data appears missing.
Important

Tracking tool—not a sportsbook

Nuke Unit Tracker does not accept wagers, deposits, or withdrawals. It does not guarantee betting outcomes. Sports betting involves risk; only participate where legal and never wager more than you can afford to lose.
