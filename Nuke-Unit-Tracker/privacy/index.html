Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Nuke Unit Tracker handles information when you use the iOS app.

1. Information you enter

Nuke Unit Tracker lets you enter betting records such as selections, sports or leagues, sportsbooks, dates, odds, unit amounts, results, notes, optional parlay legs, and optional slip photos. You may also save app preferences such as your unit value and odds format.

2. Local storage and private iCloud synchronization

Your tracker information is stored locally on your device. If private iCloud synchronization is available and enabled for the app, Apple may synchronize that information through the private CloudKit database associated with your Apple Account. Private CloudKit data is not a public feed and is not shared with other Nuke Unit Tracker users. Nuke Sports Bets does not have a tool for browsing your private tracker records or slip photos.

3. Accounts and community access

The tracker does not require a Nuke Sports Bets or Discord account. Optional community, membership, and educational links open external websites or apps. Information you provide to those services is governed by their own privacy policies and terms.

4. Analytics and advertising

The current app does not include third-party advertising or behavioral-tracking SDKs. Apple may provide developers with aggregated App Store analytics or diagnostic information according to your device settings and Apple's policies.

5. Notifications

If notification features are offered and you choose to enable them, Apple Push Notification service may process a device token and delivery information needed to send the notification. You can disable notifications at any time in iOS Settings.

6. Data control and deletion

You can edit or delete tracker entries inside the app. Nuke support cannot directly retrieve, modify, or delete information stored in your private iCloud database. Removing the app from a device may not automatically remove information already synchronized to iCloud.

7. Data security

We use the security controls provided by iOS and Apple services to protect locally stored and privately synchronized information. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Intended audience

Nuke Unit Tracker is intended for adults who are legally permitted to participate in sports betting in their jurisdiction. The app is a recordkeeping and informational tool; it does not accept wagers, deposits, or withdrawals.

9. Policy changes

We may update this policy when app features or data practices change. The effective date at the top of this page will identify the latest version.

10. Contact

Questions or privacy requests can be sent to Zodiark@nukesportsbets.com.
